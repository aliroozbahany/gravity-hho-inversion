### 🌍 About Me
I am a PhD researcher in **Geophysics / Gravimetry** with a deep passion for potential theory, geodynamics, and high-performance numerical modeling. My academic journey revolves around decoding Earth's internal structure through gravity anomalies, geoid modeling, and advanced mathematical inversion methods.

When I am not solving non-linear inverse problems or wrestling with boundary value problems (Dirichlet/Neumann), you'll find me exploring computational physics, writing clean Python code, or experimenting with simulation mechanics in **Unity (C#)**.

---

### 🔬 Research & Academic Focus
- **Potential Theory & Gravimetry:** Gravity anomalies, geoid determination, and Airy isostasy.
- **Inverse Theory:** Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm, metaheuristic optimization (e.g., Harris Hawks Optimization - HHO), and regularization techniques.
- **Geodynamics:** Advanced modeling of lithospheric dynamics and mass transport.
